Import Spotify data

Choose the JSON files from Spotify's Extended Streaming History export. You can select multiple files at once.

Your existing data will be merged. Duplicate records are ignored.
Where to get your data
  1. Open Spotify Privacy settings.
  2. Request Extended streaming history.
  3. Download and unzip the export.
  4. Select all Streaming_History_Audio_*.json files here.
WHAT GETS IMPORTED

Your listening, not your identity.

  • Track, artist and album names
  • Listening date and duration
  • Spotify track URI when available
  • Playback context where provided
